# FIXPRO.CA — MASTER WEBSITE BUILD PROMPT

Build a complete, production-ready website for **FixPro.ca**, a Canadian national repair, maintenance, and emergency service platform.

The website must feel like a substantial, established Canadian service company — not a contractor template, lead-generation landing page, directory, or startup mockup.

The primary commercial objective is:

**Generate qualified repair and maintenance requests from businesses, property managers, facility managers, multi-location organizations, and homeowners throughout Canada.**

The long-term business model is a national service network where FixPro manages the customer relationship and coordinates qualified local service providers.

---

# 1. BRAND POSITIONING

Brand:

**FixPro**

Primary domain:

**FixPro.ca**

Primary tagline:

**Get It Fixed.**

Supporting message:

**Professional repair and maintenance services across Canada.**

FixPro should occupy the space between:

- local contractors
- facility maintenance companies
- national service networks
- emergency repair companies
- handyman services

Do NOT position FixPro as simply a handyman company.

The brand should communicate:

**Whatever is broken, FixPro can coordinate the right professional to repair it.**

The customer should perceive FixPro as the primary national service provider.

Do not prominently describe FixPro as a marketplace, referral website, lead-generation website, contractor directory, or middleman.
